# Source and asset notices

The starter texts are bundled from Project Gutenberg editions that Project
Gutenberg identifies as public domain in the United States:

- *Meditations* by Marcus Aurelius,
  [eBook 2680](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2680).
- *The Notebooks of Leonardo da Vinci — Complete*, translated and edited by
  Jean Paul Richter,
  [eBook 5000](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5000).
- *The Art of War* by Sun Tzu, translated and annotated by Lionel Giles,
  [eBook 132](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/132).

The downloaded files retain the Project Gutenberg license and edition notes.
Users outside the United States should check the copyright status that applies
where they are located. Commentary from translators and editors is not treated
as the historical author's own text in Better Learning citations.

The Marcus Aurelius, Leonardo da Vinci, and Sun Tzu portraits were generated
for this project with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on September 12,
2026. They are stylized AI interpretations, not documentary likenesses. The app
labels them accordingly.

The prompt set used a consistent editorial gouache, charcoal, and printmaking
style without embedded text or watermarks:

- Marcus Aurelius as a stoic Roman emperor against a muted sage backdrop.
- Leonardo da Vinci as a Renaissance polymath with a notebook and warm apricot
  sketch backdrop.
- Sun Tzu as an ancient Chinese strategist with a bamboo manuscript and
  parchment-and-ink landscape.

Interface icons are from Lucide, available under the ISC License. Font files
are supplied through Fontsource packages for Manrope and Newsreader; consult
their packages for upstream font licenses.
