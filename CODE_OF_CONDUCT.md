# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Better Learning to be a welcoming project for learners and
contributors of every background and experience level. Participation requires
respect, patience, intellectual honesty, and care with other people's work and
personal information.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, constructive feedback and assume good faith.
- Disagree with ideas without attacking the person presenting them.
- Credit sources and contributors accurately.
- Respect privacy, consent, copyright, and repository security practices.
- Accept correction, repair mistakes, and make room for less experienced
  contributors.
- Keep project spaces focused on improving Better Learning.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
misinformation, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the project.

For a private conduct report, use
[the repository's private reporting form](https://github.com/ReservedSnow673/Better-Learning/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with “Conduct report.” Include only the details needed to
understand the incident. Maintainers may remove content, close threads, limit
participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as privately as the investigation permits. Retaliation
against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available
under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license.
